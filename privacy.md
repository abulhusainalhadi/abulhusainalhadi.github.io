# Polisi Privasi / Privacy Policy

Kemas kini: 7 November 2026

**Bahasa Melayu**

Sync Drive Peribadi ialah alat peribadi yang digunakan oleh pemiliknya sahaja (Abul Husain Al-Hadi bin Sa'ari) untuk menyalin fail kerja antara akaun Google Drive dan OneDrive miliknya sendiri. Alat ini hanya mengakses fail dalam akaun Google Drive pemilik, dengan kebenaran pemilik. Tiada data dikumpul, disimpan di pelayan lain, dijual atau dikongsi dengan mana-mana pihak ketiga. Data hanya dipindahkan antara akaun Google Drive dan OneDrive pemilik serta komputer peribadinya. Akses boleh ditarik balik pada bila-bila masa di https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

**English**

Sync Drive Peribadi is a personal tool used only by its owner to copy his own work files between his own Google Drive and OneDrive accounts. It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive files, with the owner's consent. No data is collected, stored on any other server, sold, or shared with any third party. Data moves only between the owner's own Google Drive, OneDrive and personal computer. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
